# ICOR for Life - PDF Annotation 0.2.1

- The quote and the image of a drawn box no longer merge into one blockquote.
  A new highlight leaves a blank line between them, and the image keeps its
  colored frame and stays draggable. Highlight notes you already have keep
  working and are not rewritten, so they still show the old merged layout
  until you add that blank line yourself.

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer.
