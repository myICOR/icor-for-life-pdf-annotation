# ICOR for Life - PDF Annotation 0.1.0

The first build: highlights on the built-in PDF viewer that are notes in
the vault.

- Select text, pick a colour: a highlight note with the position, the
  colour and the quote in the frontmatter and your note in the body.
- Cmd (Ctrl) plus drag for an area, saved as a PNG.
- Every highlight painted over the page; click for the toolbar, hover
  for the note, drag onto a canvas for a card of the quote or the image.
- Deep links in the viewer's own `selection=` form plus PDF++'s `rect=`
  and `color=`.
- `canvases` and `linked_notes` written into the note from the canvas
  files.
- A sidebar of the open PDF's highlights.

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer; verified against the 1.13.7 bundle. Copy
`main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` into
`<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/icor-for-life-pdf-annotation/`.
