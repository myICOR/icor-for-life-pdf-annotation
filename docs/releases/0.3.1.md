# ICOR for Life - PDF Annotation 0.3.1

0.3.0 with 0.2.1's fix, which was released on its own and missed it:

- The quote and the image of a drawn box no longer merge into one blockquote.
  A new highlight leaves a blank line between them, and the image keeps its
  coloured frame and stays draggable. Highlight notes you already have keep
  working and are not rewritten.

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer. Copy `main.js`, `manifest.json` and
`styles.css` into `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/icor-for-life-pdf-annotation/`.
