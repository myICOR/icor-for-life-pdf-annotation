# ICOR for Life - PDF Annotation 0.1.1

After Tom's first test.

- The painted layer has a size again, so highlights show.
- Swatches are round and coloured under the INKLINE theme; every plugin
  element carries `data-ink-plugin`.
- Highlights stay visible on a white page under the dark theme.
- The panel opens from the ribbon, from a button in the PDF toolbar, and
  on its own when the first PDF of a session becomes active.
- Rows with page, quote, note, hover actions and a context menu.
- Open note puts the cursor under `## Note`.
- Scanned-page quotes under a drawn box are joined or left empty.

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer; verified against the 1.13.7 bundle.
